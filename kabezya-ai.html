Know the essentials.

Practical information organized around the decisions travelers usually need to make before and during a trip.

₱

Money & budget

The Philippine peso is the local currency. Carry some cash for smaller shops and islands, and tell your bank before international card use.

✦

Weather & timing

Conditions vary by region. Build flexibility into island plans and check local forecasts and transport notices close to travel.

↗

Getting around

Domestic flights, ferries, buses, jeepneys and ride services all play a role. Leave sensible connection time between islands.

Before you go

A calmer trip starts with a clear checklist.

Kabezya AI organizes the basics so important preparation does not disappear inside dozens of tabs.

1

Check official entry requirements

Rules depend on nationality and can change. Confirm passport, visa and arrival requirements with official Philippine authorities and your airline.

2

Plan connectivity and payments

Prepare roaming or a local SIM/eSIM option, keep offline copies of bookings and carry backup payment methods.

3

Protect the flexible parts

Use appropriate travel insurance, watch weather notices and avoid tight transfers between flights, ferries or islands.

4

Travel with local respect

Dress appropriately for religious sites, ask before photographing people and follow environmental rules in protected areas.

Travel information is general guidance, not an entry guarantee. Always verify current immigration, health, weather and safety information with official sources before departure.
