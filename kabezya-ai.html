A place to explore.
A future to prepare for.

From turquoise coastlines to energetic cities, the Philippines offers many ways to begin a new chapter.

Picture weekends beside the sea, discovering local food and finding your rhythm in a new city. Palawan offers dramatic island scenery; Manila brings history and metropolitan life; Cebu blends city energy with access to coastal adventures. Choose your home base around your budget, classes and work prospects, then make time to explore.

Filipino hospitality is part of the country’s appeal. Everyday experiences still vary: people have different backgrounds, and no destination can promise every foreign visitor the same welcome. Be friendly, learn a few local greetings, respect personal boundaries and local customs, and build genuine relationships. Shared meals, language practice and community activities can help you settle in.

Know your entry route.

A clear starting point for Congolese travelers and visitors from other countries.

Visa-free does not mean unlimited

30 days
to begin.

The official Philippine visa policy lists both the Republic of the Congo and the Democratic Republic of the Congo among nationalities eligible for an initial 30-day visa-free visit, subject to entry requirements.

This is an exemption from obtaining an entry visa for an eligible short visit—not a promise of a free visa for every country or a four-month stay.
