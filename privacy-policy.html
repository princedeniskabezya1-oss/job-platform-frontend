Your privacy at AIFT

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AIFT collects, uses, protects and shares information when you use our education, employment, community, family-support and travel-guidance services.

Effective September 13, 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide when creating or using an AIFT account, such as your name, email address, account role, profile details, education or employment information, messages, applications, learning activity, support requests and content you choose to upload.

We may also collect technical information needed to operate and secure AIFT, including device and browser details, IP address, login sessions, usage events and security records.

2. Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, AIFT receives the basic information you approve, normally your name, email address and Google profile image. We use this information only to identify and securely connect you to your AIFT account. AIFT does not receive your Google password.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, personalize and maintain AIFT services.
  • Authenticate accounts and protect users from fraud or unauthorized access.
  • Support learning, job applications, professional connections and user-requested services.
  • Deliver account, security, notification and support communications.
  • Improve reliability, accessibility and user experience.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce platform policies.

4. How information is shared

Information may be visible to other users when you intentionally publish it on a profile, post, application or community feature. We may share necessary information with schools, employers, instructors, agents or service providers when you request or authorize the related service.

We use trusted infrastructure and service providers to host the frontend, backend, database, files, email and authentication. They process information only as needed to provide those services. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data security and retention

AIFT uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including password hashing, access controls and authenticated sessions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary for platform operations, security, dispute handling and legal compliance.

6. Your choices and rights

You may update applicable profile and account information through AIFT settings. You may also contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information. Some information may be retained when required for security, legal compliance or legitimate recordkeeping.

7. Children and student accounts

Student information must be provided and used with appropriate school, parent or guardian authorization where required by applicable law. AIFT does not knowingly invite children to create unauthorized independent accounts.

8. International processing

AIFT users and service providers may be located in different countries. By using the platform, information may be processed where AIFT or its providers operate, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when AIFT services or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
