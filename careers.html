A clear hiring process

If your experience matches an active need, AIFT will contact you using the email you provide.

Express interest

Tell us what you do, where you are based and how you want to contribute.

Team review

We compare your information with current and planned opportunities.

Conversation

Selected applicants discuss the role, expectations and working arrangement.

Decision

Any offer is made in writing with clear responsibilities and terms.

Stay safe: AIFT will never ask you to pay an application or recruitment fee, share a password, or send a one-time verification code.

Express your interest

Share enough information for a meaningful review. Use links for a résumé, portfolio or professional profile; do not include passwords, identity numbers or financial details.
