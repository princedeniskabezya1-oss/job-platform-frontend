The story behind AIFT · Since 2019

A dream that reaches beyond borders.

Meet Prince Denis Kabezya, the entrepreneur behind AIFT Foreign Training.

Also known online as Denis Scott — Scott is his nickname.

Prince Denis Kabezya began AIFT in 2019 with a practical ambition: help people understand their options, prepare for travel, explore work opportunities, and find a path toward education.

Turning ambition into preparation

As a businessman and the owner of AIFT Foreign Training, Denis brings an entrepreneurial approach to a personal mission. His vision includes people who find it difficult to access scholarships or who need guidance before taking a major step abroad.

That mission is about preparation: asking the right questions, understanding requirements, building language skills and approaching opportunities with a clearer plan.

AIFT’s founding idea

More people should have the guidance they need to explore learning, work and travel opportunities.

A foundation in business

Denis graduated from Treston International College with a BSBA in Marketing Management. His work with AIFT brings together his interest in business, education and helping people navigate new possibilities.

A conversation, before a commitment

For anyone considering AIFT’s Philippines program, the travel guide explains the four-month support package, what is included and what needs to be confirmed. Clear information and a written offer matter before booking or paying.
