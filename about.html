About AIFT

Education that leads to real opportunity.

AIFT is a Philippines-based education and opportunity venture. Through AIFT Foreign Training, professional preparation and our digital platform, we help people strengthen their skills, become job-ready and connect with schools, employers and communities.

Our story

Built from training experience and a belief in human potential.

AIFT began with a practical goal: help learners improve their English, professional communication and readiness for international work. That education venture grew into a broader platform connecting learning, employment, professional relationships and useful guidance.

AIFT Foreign Training

Our education initiative focuses on clear, practical learning. Students build English communication, customer service, business and workplace skills that are useful beyond a classroom. Courses are designed to be understandable, applied and connected to real goals.

Based in the PhilippinesAIFT was developed in Metro Manila and serves people with a global outlook.
Connected to diverse communitiesOur perspective is shaped by learners and professionals from different countries, languages and backgrounds.
Growing responsiblyWe continue improving the platform, partnerships and learning experience as our community develops.
